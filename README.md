# Viora Studio Community

Welcome to the official community hub for **Viora Studio**.

This is the place to connect with the people behind Viora and the wider community. Share ideas, ask questions, discuss our products, and stay up to date with what we're building.

## Join the Discussions

Use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/viora-studio/community/discussions) to take part in the conversation.

## Discussion Categories

- **Announcements:** Updates from maintainers.
- **General:** Chat about anything and everything here.
- **Ideas & Suggestions:** Share ideas for new features.
- **Polls:** Take a vote from the community.
- **Q&A:** Ask the community for help.
- **Show and tell:** Show off something you've made.

## How to Participate

- Search existing discussions before starting a new topic.
- Choose the category that best fits your post.
- Keep conversations respectful, constructive, and welcoming.
- Share enough context when asking for help.
- Never post passwords, access tokens, private keys, or other sensitive information.

## Contributing

Everyone is welcome to take part. You don't need to be a developer to contribute. Feedback, questions, ideas, and thoughtful conversations all help the Viora community grow.

## About Viora Studio

Viora Studio builds innovative digital products and experiences designed to make technology faster, more accessible, and more enjoyable.

We believe technology should do more than solve problems. It should inspire, connect, and create new possibilities.
